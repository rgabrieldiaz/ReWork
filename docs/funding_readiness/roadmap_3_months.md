- [ ] **Mainnet Contract Deployment:** Deploy and verify Trustless Work V1 production contracts on Stellar Mainnet.
- [ ] **Audit Bank Review:** Leverage SDF Audit Bank partner to audit contract interactions and transaction signing flows.
- [ ] **SEP-0007 Mobile Polishing:** Finalize deep-linking handlers for Lobstr and Freighter Mobile apps on iOS/Android.
- [ ] **Wallet Auto-Funding Flow:** Integrated friendbot/onboarding helper for test environments and warning banners for real funds.

- **Pilot Launch:** Onboard 2 anchor organizations (1 digital software agency + 1 Web3 builder community).

## 📈 Month 2 (November 2026): SCF 7.0 Build Award — Tranche #1 (20%)
**Primary Focus:** Local Anchor Integrations, Fiat Ramps & Mobile PWA.

### Technical Deliverables
- [ ] **Stellar Anchor Integration (SEP-0024):** Direct on/off-ramp from Argentine Pesos (ARS) to native Stellar USDC via licensed local anchors.
- [ ] **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Offline caching, push notifications for milestone approvals, and biometric login.
- [ ] **Automated Invoice & Receipt Generator:** PDF generation with on-chain transaction hash verification for legal/tax accounting.
- [ ] **Realtime Event Indexer:** Dedicated RPC listener for Soroban escrow events (`funded`, `released`, `disputed`).

### Business & Traction KPIs
- **Workspace Growth:** 10 active business workspaces.
- **Target Users:** 350 active contractors and clients.
- **Cumulative On-Chain Volume:** USD $50,000 in escrow disbursements.
